GRIDPULSE

Hit the lit cell. Miss and it stays lit. 60 seconds. Maximize your bit rate.

  1. One cell lights up. Press that key.
  2. A miss keeps the target lit. You still have to hit it, and a miss cancels a hit, so accuracy is worth as much as speed.
  3. Targets are drawn uniformly at random, with replacement. The same cell can come up twice in a row. There is no pattern to learn.

The grid follows QWERTY keyboard rows.
